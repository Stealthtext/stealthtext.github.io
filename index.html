What is text steganography?

Text steganography hides information in the structure or choice of words in apparently ordinary text. Unlike encryption, its goal is to conceal that a message exists.

Does this replace encryption?

No. Steganography and encryption solve different problems. This demonstration encrypts the secret before mapping the ciphertext to words.

How can my team evaluate StealthText?

Start with a use case, define what must survive editing or transmission, then test extraction accuracy, detectability, and security. A production REST API is not included in this browser demo.

Are these real customer case studies?

No. The scenarios shown here are illustrative, not verified customer deployments or security results.
